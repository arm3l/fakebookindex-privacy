# Privacy Policy — Fake Book Index

*Last updated: March 2026*

## Data collection
Fake Book Index does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Local storage
The app stores data locally on your device only:
- The jazz standards index (downloaded from GitHub on first launch)
- Your filter preferences
- Your custom book indexes
- Your language preference

This data never leaves your device.

## Internet connection
The app connects to the internet only once, on first launch, to download the index from github.com/milnak/FakeBookIndex. After that, it works fully offline.

The Update button triggers a new download from GitHub. No other network requests are made.

## Third party data
The index data is sourced from milnak/FakeBookIndex, distributed under CC0 license (public domain).
